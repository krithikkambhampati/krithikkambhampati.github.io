A full-stack platform for running events end-to-end — registration, merchandise, attendance, and live discussion — with role-based access throughout.

Overview

Three distinct roles — Admin, Organizer, Participant — each see a different slice of the same platform, enforced both in the UI and at the API layer so permissions can't be bypassed by hitting an endpoint directly.

Merchandise & payments

A payment-approval workflow sits in front of merchandise checkout, with atomic stock decrements so two participants can't both successfully purchase the last unit of an item under concurrent load.

Attendance

QR-based check-in gives organizers a live view of scan status as attendees arrive, with a one-click CSV export for post-event reporting.

Community layer

  • A real-time, per-event discussion forum built on WebSockets, so conversation stays scoped to the event it belongs to.
  • @everyone broadcast alerts for organizers to push announcements to every participant at once.
  • Two-layer bot protection via Google reCAPTCHA v2/v3 to keep the forum spam-free without adding friction for real users.
